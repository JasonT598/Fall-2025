Assassin's Creed Martian- A Quake 4 Mod

This is the Assassin's Creed Martian(AC Martian) Mod for Quake 4, turning a first-person shooter into a first-person shooter focused on stealth and since the planet is red with Aliens, I decided it was Mars.
Nothing more, nothing less

How to Play:
All of your allies are stationary, and now you only have a blaster and your hands.
You must sneak through the levels, taking down enemies via a stealth takedown or
with your silenced blaster. The enemies will be passive and or stationary when walking around.
Until they see you, then they'll start firing at you. You must hide behind walls and or gates.
To get the chance to take down your enemies and slip by onto the next level, you need to get help from
your allies for health and other stuff.

Tips:
Hide behind walls to stay out of the enemy's sight
Crouch behind smaller walls to also stay out of sight
Time your stealth takedowns or movements carefully to slip by.
Try not to be spotted; they can kill you much quicker than you can kill them
